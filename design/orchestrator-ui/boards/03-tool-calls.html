One row, four answersStatus square, tool icon, verb, target. Everything to the right of the target is that tool’s own micrographic.
Three openingsThe three squares at the right edge show and set how open a call is: one line, a summary, or everything.
ColourEach tool owns a muted hue on its icon, verb and border. Status keeps its own: violet wash while working, amber and a cut corner when it needs you, red for failure. Tool hues steer clear of those three.
OrchestrationSpawn, Message, Wait and Result reuse the tree’s harness glyphs and the ledger’s step track, so agents look the same everywhere.
